Cloud and alternative host agents running the ticket workflow follow `docs/cloud-workflow.md` and the matching adapter in `workflow/adapters/`: `codex.md` or `agy.md` for cloud hosts, `codex-local.md` or `opencode-local.md` for local interactive hosts.
